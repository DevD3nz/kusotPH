The story

KusotPh didn't start in an office.
It started at a laundry counter, on the customer's side.

  1. 01

    “Balikan n'yo na lang po bukas.”

    I dropped off a full bag of clothes at my neighborhood laundry shop and paid. I asked when I could get it. “Bukas po siguro, hapon.” Tomorrow, maybe, sometime in the afternoon. No exact time and no number to text. All I had was a small paper stub.

  2. 02

    Two trips for one bag.

    The next afternoon I walked back. “Dinadryer pa po.” Still drying. Nobody had told me, because nobody could. I went home empty-handed, came back the day after, and went to work in yesterday's shirt. It wasn't the shop's fault. They had no way to reach me.

  3. 03

    Now look at it from your side of the counter.

    Every customer like me costs your shop something.

    • A phone call your attendant answers while a load waits to be folded.
    • A bag on the shelf that nobody comes back for, taking up space for weeks.
    • A customer who quietly tries the shop down the street next time, and never tells you why.

    You won't see it in your sales. You'll just have fewer suki than you should.

  4. 04

    So I built the shop I wished I'd walked into.

    Kusot is how we scrub clothes by hand: simple, hands-on, done by the person at the tub. KusotPh works the same way. Your attendant takes the order on a phone, and from that moment the customer knows what's happening:

    • A ready-by time printed on every claim ticket.
    • A live receipt link that shows Received → Washing → Drying → Ready.
    • A text the moment it's Ready, plus reminders if the bag is still waiting after 3, 7 and 30 days.
  5. 05

    Then I built the rest of your shop around it.

    Clear updates for customers need clean records behind the counter. So KusotPh also keeps the cash drawer honest, tracks who worked which shift, and lets you see every branch from your phone, without driving between them to ask.

How it works

One board. Every load. Always in the right stage.

Your attendants move each order along with a tap. The customer's link updates as they go.

  1. 0

    To collect

    Home pickup: a rider brings the dirty laundry in.

  2. 1

    Received

    Weighed, priced, ticket printed with a ready-by time.

  3. 2

    Washing

    In the machine. Flagged Late if the time passes.

  4. 3

    Drying

    Almost there. The customer can see it too.

  5. 4

    Ready

    Customer gets a text. Reminders after 3, 7, 30 days.

  6. 5

    Claimed / Delivered

    Released only once the balance is zero.

  • Undo a step within 15 minutes
  • Failed delivery goes back to Ready, attempt counted
  • Cancel only by owner or manager, with a reason
  • Decimal kilos or per piece, plus rush & delivery fees

Questions

Before you ask.

Do I need a computer or a POS machine?

No. KusotPh runs in the browser on any phone or tablet and can be installed to the home screen like an app. A thermal printer is optional, for claim tickets.

How do customers get the text when their laundry is ready?

Through the SMS add-on. Once it's switched on for your shop, KusotPh texts customers when their order reaches Ready and reminds them if it's still unclaimed after 3, 7 and 30 days.

Does it take GCash payments?

KusotPh records payments made by cash, GCash, bank transfer or other methods against each order, including deposits and cash on delivery. It is not a payment gateway; your customers pay you directly the way they do now.

I have more than one branch. Will it work?

Yes. Each branch has its own orders, cash drawer and staff, and the owner sees all of them together in the Command Center.

My attendants aren't techy.

The order board is built for the counter: big buttons, PIN login, one tap to move a load to the next stage. I'll walk your team through it during setup.
